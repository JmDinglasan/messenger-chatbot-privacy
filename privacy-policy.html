Privacy Policy for CarDealerBot

Last updated: November 9, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how this Facebook Messenger chatbot (“we”, “our”, or “the chatbot”) handles information received from users through the Messenger platform.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or share any personal information. Messages sent to this chatbot are processed through Facebook’s Messenger API only to generate automated replies. No information is stored outside of Facebook’s systems.

How We Use Information

The chatbot uses message content solely to understand and respond appropriately to user inquiries. We do not use user data for analytics, advertising, or marketing purposes.

Data Retention

No user data is permanently stored or shared with third parties. All message processing happens within Facebook’s secure environment.

Children’s Privacy

This chatbot does not knowingly interact with or collect data from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
