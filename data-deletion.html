PhilCare Chatbot - Data Deletion Instructions Last Updated: July 24, 2026 This page explains how you can request the deletion of your personal data collected through the PhilCare Facebook Messenger chatbot ("Phil"). What data is collected When you message the PhilCare Facebook Page, we may store your Facebook name, profile picture, and the messages you exchange with the chatbot or a live PhilCare support agent. This information is used only to provide customer support and is not used for advertising or shared with third parties. How to request deletion To request that your data be deleted, you may do either of the following: - Send an email to supportservices@philcare.com.ph with the subject line "Chatbot Data Deletion Request," including the name and/or Facebook account you used to message PhilCare. - Call the PhilCare hotline at (02) 8462-1800 and ask to have your chatbot conversation data deleted. Processing time PhilCare will process and confirm your data deletion request within a reasonable timeframe in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Once processed, your stored Messenger conversation history and associated profile information will be permanently deleted from PhilCare's systems. Questions For any questions about this process or PhilCare's data privacy practices, please contact supportservices@philcare.com.ph or callcenter@philcare.com.ph.
